The sidebar page has two new tabs, OpenCode and Shell, next to Connect. OpenCode runs OpenCode's terminal app, connected to the app's own server, so it shares sessions with the web app and your paired devices. Shell is a terminal in /data/home. The page opens on the tab you used last, and the terminals keep running when you leave it, so you can pick up where you left off, even from another browser.
